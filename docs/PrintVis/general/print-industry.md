# Introduction to the Print Industry

PrintVis is an integration with Business Central to provide a MIS/ERP solution built as a vertical in the print industry. Having knowledge of the print industry before working with PrintVis is beneficial for both setup and day-to-day use.

## Usage

The print industry consists of a vast category of products, including books, magazines, labels, packaging, brochures, business cards, and much more. Terminology and lingo will vary depending on the print shop and what they produce, but many words are universal between shops.

Understanding the following core areas will help you get the most from PrintVis:

- **Printing processes** — flexography, packaging, lithography, digital, screen printing, inkjet, letterpress, gravure, etc.
- **Finishing processes** — cutting, folding, binding, laminating, die-cutting, etc.
- **Materials** — paper, substrates, inks, coatings, plates, etc.
- **Industry terminology** — sheets, signatures, impositions, make-ready, residual sheets, grain direction, color coverage, etc.

Each printing method has benefits and challenges when it comes to producing products. Understanding these helps when setting up cost centers, estimation, and production workflows in PrintVis.
