# Requisition Worksheet

The Requisition Worksheet in Business Central is used to create purchase orders for materials needed in production. When used in conjunction with the PrintVis Purchase Guide and Material Requirements, it provides a streamlined purchasing workflow.

## Usage

The Requisition Worksheet is used by purchasing staff to:
- Review suggested purchase orders based on production needs and reorder points
- Add or modify purchase requisitions before converting them to purchase orders
- Calculate suggested quantities based on minimum order quantities and demand
- Generate purchase orders from the worksheet

## Setup

The Requisition Worksheet follows standard Business Central functionality. Setup involves:
- Reorder points and safety stock on items
- Vendor setup with minimum order quantities and lead times
- Replenishment method on item cards
