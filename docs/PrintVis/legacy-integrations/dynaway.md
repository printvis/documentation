# Dynaway Integration

Dynaway is an enterprise asset management (EAM) solution for Business Central. The PrintVis Dynaway integration connects PrintVis production management with Dynaway's equipment maintenance capabilities.

## Usage

The Dynaway integration is used to:
- Trigger maintenance work orders from PrintVis when equipment needs service
- Track planned maintenance against production schedules
- Integrate production downtime recording with maintenance workflows
- Manage spare parts and maintenance costs linked to print equipment
