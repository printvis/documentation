# Alternative Rates

Alternative Rates in PrintVis allow you to define different pricing scenarios within a calculation unit — for example, offering a standard rate and a rush rate, or pricing for different customer tiers without creating separate calculation units.

## Usage

Alternative Rates are assigned to Calculation Unit lines and provide a way to switch between different pricing scenarios during estimation. The estimator can select the appropriate alternative rate for a given job.

Common uses:
- Rush pricing vs. standard pricing for the same operation
- Customer-tier pricing (e.g., preferred customer rate vs. standard rate)
- Seasonal or promotional pricing
- Minimum charge rates vs. variable rates

## Setup

Alternative Rates are found by searching **PrintVis Alternative Rates**.
